Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Stream Auto Clipper · Effective date: 2026-11-15

GTA 6 Stream Auto Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool run by its owner on the owner's own laptop. It makes YouTube Shorts from the owner's own gameplay streams and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

  • The app requests one Google permission only: the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos.
  • It does not read your email, contacts, comments, watch history, subscribers or any other Google or YouTube account data.

How the data is used

  • The permission is used only to upload Shorts, with their title, description and hashtags, to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • The app never uploads to, or acts on, anyone else's channel.

Storage

  • The OAuth sign-in token is stored locally on the owner's laptop, in the app's own folder. It is never uploaded to any server.
  • Video clips and logs are also kept only on that laptop.

Sharing

  • No data is shared, sold, rented or sent anywhere other than YouTube itself, as part of the upload.
  • The app and this website use no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.

How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access at any time: open your Google Account, go to Security, then Third-party access (also called "Your connections to third-party apps & services"), select the app and remove its access. You can also visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the laptop also stops the app from uploading.

Google and YouTube policies

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube through this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

GTA 6 Stream Auto Clipper

A personal tool that turns the owner's own GTA 6 gameplay streams into YouTube Shorts.

What it does

  • Records the owner's own live gameplay stream on the owner's laptop.
  • Finds the most exciting moments and cuts them into short vertical clips.
  • Uploads those clips as Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who it is for

This is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public and it only ever acts on the owner's own YouTube channel. It asks Google for one permission only: uploading videos (the youtube.upload scope).

Privacy

The sign-in token stays on the owner's laptop and no data is shared with anyone. Read the full privacy policy.
